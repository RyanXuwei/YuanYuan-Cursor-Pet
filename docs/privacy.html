隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 21 日 · 適用目前 v1.13.0 的資料處理方式

YuanYuan Cursor Pet 在本機處理互動與儲存設定，不傳送滑鼠活動或本機資料給開發者，不含廣告追蹤或使用分析。寵物消息預設關閉；主動啟用後才連線 Google News RSS，傳送固定搜尋條件與語言／新聞版。

1. 本政策適用範圍

本政策說明 BitingBox Studio 的 YuanYuan Cursor Pet Chrome 擴充功能如何處理資料。它在一般網頁中顯示互動角色，依使用者選擇的行動方針自主活動。

2. 在本機處理的互動資訊

為提供角色跟隨、拎起晃動、投球、拖動飯碗及點擊泡泡等功能，擴充功能會即時處理滑鼠／指標位置、移動、點擊及拖曳。動畫也會使用可視區尺寸、分頁可見性和全螢幕狀態。

這些資訊僅在裝置記憶體中短暫使用。例如，投球時會保留近期少量拖曳取樣以計算速度；不建立持久的活動紀錄，不傳送至開發者或外部伺服器，也不用於辨識、分析或追蹤使用者。

3. 儲存在裝置的偏好設定

擴充功能使用 chrome.storage.local 儲存：

  • 角色顯示開關與大小。
  • 行動方針及允許出現的行為。
  • 介面語言及設定格式版本。
  • 你主動加入狗籠名單的網站主機名稱（網域），用於在指定網站隱藏角色。

這些資料保存在目前 Chrome 設定檔的本機儲存區；擴充功能不使用 chrome.storage.sync 將其同步至 Google 帳號，也不將其傳送給開發者。

狗籠名單不會自動建立造訪歷程，只記錄你主動選擇的網域，不儲存完整網址、路徑、查詢參數或瀏覽時間。

4. 不會擷取的資料

擴充功能不擷取網頁文字、圖片或表單輸入作為使用者資料，不讀取瀏覽紀錄、密碼、付款資訊、個人聯絡資料或地理位置，也不使用相機或麥克風。網站存取權用於顯示角色、處理上述互動與判斷全螢幕狀態。

5. 分享、廣告與外部服務

本擴充功能不出售或出租資料，不將本機互動、寵物圖片或已讀紀錄分享給第三方，不含分析 SDK、廣告追蹤或遠端執行程式碼。程式與角色素材隨套件提供。選用寵物消息的對外搜尋請求另於下方說明。

關於頁另提供自願支持的「Give Me A Boba!」外部連結（bitingbox.bobaboba.me）。只有使用者點擊時才開啟新分頁，擴充功能不傳送互動資料、付款資料或付款狀態，亦不在內部處理付款；後續資料處理由贊助平台及其支付服務依各自政策辦理。按鈕圖片包含在套件內，不自動從外部載入。

關於頁及本政策含 GitHub 連結；使用者主動開啟外部網站後，該網站依其自身政策處理資料。若你在 GitHub 提交問題，內容依該平台的公開設定處理，請勿在回報中加入密碼、住址或其他不想公開的資訊。

6. 控制與移除

你可在設定面板停用角色、限制允許行為及修改偏好，也可透過 Chrome 的擴充功能設定限制網站存取或移除本擴充功能。移除擴充功能會清除其對應的本機儲存設定。

7. 本政策網站

本頁由 GitHub Pages 託管。造訪本頁時，GitHub 可能依其隱私聲明處理網站連線與服務紀錄；這與擴充功能內的本機互動處理不同。本頁沒有另加分析、追蹤或第三方字型。

8. 政策更新與聯絡

若資料處理方式改變，我們會更新本頁日期與內容，並視適用要求更新商店揭露或提供通知。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Reflects the data practices of v1.13.0

YuanYuan Cursor Pet processes interactions and stores preferences locally, with no mouse activity or local data sent to the developer, advertising tracking or analytics. Optional pet news is off by default. When enabled, it sends predefined search terms and language/news-edition parameters to Google News RSS.

1. Scope

This policy describes how BitingBox Studio's YuanYuan Cursor Pet Chrome extension handles data. The extension displays an interactive character on ordinary web pages and chooses activities according to your preferences.

2. Interaction data processed locally

The extension processes pointer positions, movement, clicks and dragging to support following, picking up the pet, throwing the ball, moving the bowl and popping bubbles. Animation also uses viewport dimensions, tab visibility and fullscreen state.

This information is used temporarily in device memory. For example, a small recent sample of drag events is retained to calculate throw velocity. The extension does not create a persistent activity history, transmit this information to the developer or external servers, or use it to identify, analyze or track you.

3. Preferences stored on your device

The extension uses chrome.storage.local for:

  • Pet visibility and size.
  • Behavior style and allowed activities.
  • Interface language and settings schema version.
  • Website hostnames (domains) you explicitly add to the cage list to hide the pet on those websites.

These preferences remain in the local storage of your current Chrome profile. The extension does not use chrome.storage.sync to sync them with a Google account or send them to the developer.

The cage list does not automatically build a browsing history. It stores only domains you explicitly select, not full URLs, paths, query parameters or visit times.

4. Data not extracted

The extension does not extract web page text, images or form entries as user data. It does not read browsing history, passwords, payment information, personal contacts or geolocation, and does not use the camera or microphone. Website access is used to display the pet, handle the interactions described above and detect fullscreen state.

5. Sharing, advertising and external services

The extension does not sell or rent data, or share local interactions, pet images or read markers with third parties. It contains no analytics SDKs, advertising tracking or remotely executed code. Code and character assets are bundled. Optional outbound news search requests are described below.

The About panel also offers an optional "Give Me A Boba!" link to bitingbox.bobaboba.me. It opens a new tab only when clicked. The extension does not send interaction data, payment details or payment status to this service and does not process payments. Subsequent data handling is governed by the support platform and its payment providers. The button image is bundled locally and does not load automatically from a remote server.

The About panel and this policy link to GitHub. When you choose to open an external website, that website's policies apply. Any issue you submit to GitHub is handled according to that platform's visibility settings; do not include passwords, home addresses or other information you do not want made public.

6. Your controls

You can disable the pet, restrict allowed activities and change preferences in the settings panel. Chrome's extension settings let you restrict website access or uninstall the extension. Uninstalling removes the extension's associated local settings.

7. This policy website

This page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection and service logs under its Privacy Statement when you visit this page; this is separate from the extension's local interaction processing. This page adds no analytics, tracking or third-party fonts.

8. Updates and contact

If data practices change, we will update this page and its date, and update store disclosures or provide notices as applicable.

自訂寵物圖片 / Custom pet images

自行匯入的寵物圖片、名稱與生成描述僅儲存在裝置本機的擴充功能 IndexedDB；啟用資訊儲存在本機設定。滑鼠位置、點擊與拖曳僅即時處理，不建立持久操作紀錄，也不傳送外部伺服器。匯入頁不會自動將圖片或提示詞傳給 ChatGPT；若自行貼到第三方服務，適用該服務的政策。匯出的備份包含圖片，請自行妥善保存。解除安裝會清除擴充功能本機資料，請先備份。

Imported pet images, names and descriptions stay in the extension’s local IndexedDB. Active selection stays in local settings. Mouse interactions are processed in real time without persistent activity logs or external transmission. Nothing is automatically sent to ChatGPT. Exported backups contain your images; keep them private. Uninstalling clears local extension data, so export first.

每日寵物消息與本機翻譯 / Pet news and local translation

寵物消息預設關閉。主動啟用並授權 Google News 後，背景程序才以固定關鍵字請求 https://news.google.com/rss/search。請求包含主題、語言及新聞版；Google 會收到 IP 等一般連線資訊。請求不夾帶 Cookie，不傳送目前頁面網址、內容、滑鼠活動、寵物圖片或已讀狀態。開發者沒有共用新聞或使用者資料伺服器。

新聞標題、來源、連結、發布／更新時間、已讀與開關只存 chrome.storage.local。同一 Chrome 設定檔的分頁共享，不跨電腦／設定檔同步。快取最多五個語言版本各兩則目前結果；已讀最多 300 個標記，標記時清理 14 天前項目。關閉功能停止新請求並隱藏消息，本機資料仍保留；解除安裝會移除。點原文另開外部網站後，適用該網站政策。

選用 Chrome Translator API 僅在使用者點擊且本機模型已備妥時翻譯標題，不使用 AI 搜尋、摘要或醫療內容判斷，不自動下載模型，也不呼叫外部 AI API。

Pet news is off by default. After you enable it and grant access, the background script requests Google News RSS with predefined keywords and language/news-edition parameters. Google receives normal connection information such as your IP address. Requests omit cookies and do not send current page URLs or content, mouse activity, pet images or read status. There is no developer-operated shared news or user-data server.

Article metadata, update status, read markers and settings stay in chrome.storage.local. Tabs in one Chrome profile share them; different devices and profiles do not sync. Cache holds up to two current articles for each of five language settings. Read markers are capped at 300, with entries older than 14 days cleared when marking an article read. Turning news off stops requests and hides the carrier while retaining local data; uninstalling removes it. External article pages follow their own policies.

Optional Chrome Translator title translation runs only on explicit interaction with an already available local model. It does not perform AI search, summaries or medical assessment, automatically download models or call external AI APIs.
